# Datasets

| Dataset | Description | Authors/Publishers | Source/Repository | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **AmazonHistoryPrice** | 930 products and their real prices sourced from CamelCamelCamel across 18 product categories. Each product includes the name, description, features, lowest and highest price, current price, list price, and an image link. | Tian Xia, Zhiwei He, Tong Ren, Yibo Miao, Zhuosheng Zhang, Yang Yang*, Rui Wang* | [Publication](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.213.pdf) / [Repository](https://github.com/TianXiaSJTU/AmazonPriceHistory) | [Apache 2.0 License](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) |
| **Best Offer Sequential Bargaining** | Sequential bargaining data from eBay's Best Offer feature, containing negotiation sequences between buyers and sellers including product listings, listed prices, buyer offers, seller counteroffers, and deal outcomes. | NBER | [NBER](https://www.nber.org/research/data/best-offer-sequential-bargaining) | Public for research (citation required) |
| **CaSiNo: A Corpus of Campsite Negotiation Dialogues for Automatic Negotiation Systems** | Dialogue of 1030 negotiations where two participants take the roles of campsite neighbors and negotiate for resources (food, water, firewood). | Kushal Chawla, Jaysa Ramirez, Rene Clever, Gale Lucas, Jonathan May, Jonathan Gratch | [Publication](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.254/) / [ConvoKit](https://convokit.cornell.edu/documentation/casino-corpus.html) | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| **Deal or No Deal Negotiation Dataset** | Multi-issue bargaining dialogues where two agents negotiate to split a set of items (books, hats, balls) with different values. Includes data from Netherlands (51), Germany (47), and the United States (53). | Thierry Post, Martijn J. van den Assem, Guido Baltussen, Richard H. Thaler | [GitHub](https://github.com/facebookresearch/end-to-end-negotiator) | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| **Job Interview/Negotiation** | Human-human negotiation dialogue corpus in a job interview scenario featuring dialogue act-based breakdown detection. | Junya Mori, Keiichiro Ono, Yasuhiro Sogawa | [Publication](https://aclanthology.org/2021.eacl-main.63/) / [GitHub](https://github.com/gucci-j/negotiation-breakdown-detection) | [MIT License](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT) |
| **CRA (Multi-Issue Negotiation)** | 134 human-human negotiation dialogues (11k utterances) where two participants negotiate over 1 painting, 2 lamps, and 3 records with different point values. Annotated with dialogue acts (make/ask offer, accept, reject, ask/share preference) and item allocations. Data parsed by Eleanor Lin and Kushal Chawla. | David DeVault, Johnathan Mell, Jonathan Gratch | [Publication](https://aaai.org/papers/10335-10335-toward-natural-turn-taking-in-a-virtual-human-negotiation-agent/) / [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/295854474) | Research use (contact authors) |
| **StanfordNLP - Craigslist Bargains** | Dialogues between two agents, a buyer and a seller, that negotiate over the price of an item for sale on Craigslist. | He He, Derek Chen, Anusha Balakrishnan, Percy Liang | [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/datasets/stanfordnlp/craigslist_bargains) / [Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.09637) | [MIT License](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT) |
